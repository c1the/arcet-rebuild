FREEDONIA HERALD — 28 May 2001

Backbenchers circulate letter urging leadership vote

A group of Unity Party backbenchers has begun circulating a letter calling
for a vote on Alexandra Voss's leadership, senior party sources said last
night. The letter is understood to carry roughly thirty signatures, short
of the forty-five required to trigger a formal challenge under party rules.

Allies of Ms Voss dismissed the effort as "a noisy minority", pointing to
her cabinet's unified public statements this week. Organisers claim more
signatures will follow after Thursday's by-election result.
